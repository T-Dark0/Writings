# Chapter 1
## A goddess' reasonable path to love

Sanae was walking on her own towards the Moriya shrine, her head looking down, partly to avoid tripping on the barely-trodden path but mostly as a reflection of her emotional state. She had been walking for a couple hours, and had reached the Yokai forest, putting her about halfway between her starting point in the Human Village and her home. Truthfully, she realized it would have been much faster to fly, not to mention she would have been far less fatigued by the time she had to fly up Yokai Mountain, but she was hoping to clear her head. Kanako and Suwako would certainly have tried to help if they'd known, and she was thoroughly unwilling to sit through _that_ awkwardness. So, she walked. Sad, slightly tired, and... wait, were those footsteps?

As it turned out, they were, for Komachi was walking at a rapid pace towards the Human Village. This filled Sanae with concern: one does not typically react with nonchalance at the sight of the embodiment of death, complete with scythe, marching towards the largest group of mortals in the land. Thus, the shrine maiden asked with concern, as the shinigami went by: "Komachi, is everything ok?"  
Komachi looked almost surprised to have been spoken to for a moment, but she quickly recomposed herself: "Oh, hi, Sanae. Don't worry, nobody has died at the Human Village. My boss just sent me to "do my job", nevermind nobody's dead and I was comfortably resting, and then I heard Tenshi was at the village and decided to give that girl another lesson."  
"Didn't you do that already? Actually, why is she even alive?"  
"I don't have the authority to take lives, I merely ferry the dead across the Sanzu river. And yes, I did defeat Tenshi. That doesn't mean I can't do it again, that girl really does not deserve her immortality.", Komachi replied, surprising vitriol tingeing her last few words. "Hey, you're coming from that direction, is she actually at the village?"  
"Uh, I don't know. I didn't run into her, but I didn't ask either. If the was, she probably came to buy something for her girlfriend. Poor Shion can't exactly enter the human village harmlessly."  
"I suppose that's true", said Komachi with resignation. "Hey, hang on a minute, you're not carrying anything. What did you go to do?"  
This question caught Sanae completely flat-footed. Truthfully, she'd gone to try to see Reimu, as she knew the other shrine maiden was sometimes seen in the human village just before the full moon, but she could never _admit_ that. "Uh, ah, I was... looking for Kaguya?" Immediately she cursed inwardly at the weakness of the excuse.  
"Right you were. Kaguya. The moon princess. Who doesn't even _visit_ the Human Village." Komachi, unsurprisingly, spotted the lie immediately. After a few moments of awkard silence she continued "Hey, it's ok, I promise I won't tell anyone."  
"Really?" Sanae replied immediately.  
"Really. I'm neutral. That's the one part of my job I take seriously all the time."  
Telling the great equalizer about her love problems seemed strange, Sanae reflected, but she was right, neutrality was the single most important characteristic of a shinigami. Besides, Komachi was notoriously a slacker, and not the type of person to spread Sanae's secrets. "Uh, I was hoping I'd run into Reimu"  
"Ahh, so you've joined the club."  
"The club?!" Sanae responded with a mixture of confusion and anxiety. Were other people also in love with Reimu?  
"The club of Gensokyo girls who are in love with each other, of course. Its current membership includes the three magicians and their little demon, Shion and... Tenshi, and now you"  
Sanae was sometimes known as the green shrine maiden, but in that moment she felt herself become a brighter shade of red than Reimu's outfit. Why, if Mokou had been around, the immortal might have asked her how she was outshining her fire so brightly. "wh- wh- what!?!?"  
Komachi laughed: "You tried to keep this secret, then turned a bit red as you told me, and now you've all but confirmed it. I've had a lot of practice teasing secrets from humans, though usually they're ghosts and I'm trying to convince them it's time to pass on. Let me guess, she has no idea. Though, this being Reimu, I can't say I blame you."  
Sanae replied glumly: "Yeah, I think the same thing. It's too bad, really."  
"Too bad? Girl, you perform miracles! Surely you can do _something_ about this."  
"Absolutely not!" answered Sanae with enough force to surprise Komachi. "Yes, I may have the power to do it, but I can't brainwash her into loving me, that would be unforgivable!"  
"Hmm. I suppose you're right." Komachi paused for a moment, seemingly lost in thought. "You could do this over time and tell her, though"  
"What?" Sanae said, this time with confusion.  
"If she knew, she could tell you to stop whenever. If she doesn't, clearly she's ok with it. And if you do this slowly, she will have time to notice it happen and change her mind"  
Sanae spent a few seconds on that thought, visibly perking up as she did so. Then, she almost laughed as she spoke: "Yes. Yes! I could do that! It's perfect! Thank you so much, Komachi!"  
"See? It wasn't that difficult", Komachi smiled, "You should have told someone sooner. Surely the goddesses of your shrine could have helped"  
"Maybe? It feels like I'd get an awkward talk that means to help but doesn't really if I did that, and I assume you can imagine I'm not really up for that."  
"Can I ever" Komachi sighed. "My boss keeps bothering me, I wish she'd just leave me alone already. I got distracted _one time_ and since then she doesn't even let me lie down for a while while nobody is dying." She then looked around herself for a moment, before fixing her gaze on a direction with a smirk.  
"Uh, what's over there?" Asked Sanae.  
"Oh, nothing. It's just that there's a large rock covered in a thick cushion of moss a few hundred meters in that direction. I've rested on it before, and I don't think the boss knows about it."  
"Really? You have secret resting spots?"  
"Of course I do."  
"Right. Where are the others?"  
"Well, I can't tell you about all of them, but..." Komachi replied. It made for a pleasant conversation, and Sanae resumed her journey some ten minutes later, this time by flying, heading directly for the Hakurei shrine.  

It was as the shrine came into view that Sanae's anxieties finally crystallized, and the concern paralyzed her. What if Reimu said no immediately? What if Reimu reacted with anger? What if the was crossing a line? Onozu... Komachi may have been suprisingly helpful, but what does death know of the loves of the living? Sanae found herself doing the aerial equivalent of pacing, flying back and forth between the direction of the Hakurei Shrine and that of Yokai Mountain. More than once she was on the verge of going home, and more than once she stopped herself by saying she couldn't just give up like that. Eventually, an idea came to mind. Sanae thought of asking someone who would definitely know Reimu well. Someone who had herself gotten past her romantic hangups and formed a polycule with her loved ones. Why of course, Marisa Kirisame. Surely she would have some advice for Sanae. Yes, it did involve telling the magician about a personal secret of hers, but for all her thieving habits, Marisa had never been known to engage in blackmail or to share secrets easily. It should be safe enough, Sanae told herself. Then, a resolution reached, she turned south, towards the Forest of Magic, and just a little bit east, towards Marisa's home.

Upon arriving, Sanae was greeted by two unpleasant discoveries:  
One, the witch was a colossal hoarder. Sanae had heard Marisa's house was messy, but that didn't even _begin_ to describe the transcendental chaos of her house. The sheer madness of the layout of its contents. The clear unsafety in the arrangement of most of them, and the way one had to wonder if the house was bigger on the inside in some way it was hard to be sure of. If she hadn't known goddesses, she would have thought Marisa was obviously a goddess of chaos, enemy of all that is organized. As her knowledge stood, Sanae decided it was far more likely most of those were accidental deathtraps for would-be burglars.  
Two, Marisa wasn't home. Which, thinking about it, was more important.  
Fortunately, Sanae didn't have to look around much. She just had to call the "ordinary magician"'s name once for her to reply from behind the temple of disorgan... ahem, the house, and make her way around, carrying a basket full of mushrooms.  
"Hello Sanae! Have you come to visit Gensokyo's greatest magician, ze?"  
"I wonder what would happen if your girlfriends heard you say that."  
"Last time, they unleashed Koakuma on me. I'm not sure I wouldn't be up for that again, honest."  
"I... see.", replied Sanae, slightly taken aback by the other girl's openness.  
"Well, anyway, did you want something? Mushroom?" Marisa asked, taking a mushroom from her basket and pointing it in Sanae's direction  
"Uh, are those poisonous?"  
"Yeah. Still edible though."  
"Thank you, but I think I'll pass." Sanae said while internally wondering how exactly Marisa was still alive and hadn't killed anyone "I actually wanted to ask you something. It's... it's about Reimu."  
"Oh? Is that why you're turning red?"  
Sanae suppressed the instinct to hide her blush, it was too late. She didn't suppress the thought that apparently she kept running into annoyingly perceptive people that day, though. "In a way. I wanted to... to ask her for permission to cast... a miracle on her."  
"Oh? What kinda miracle, ze?"  
"I..." Oh, damn the hesitation, it was too late to stop anyway "I fell for her a while ago, and I'd like to make her return that. Slowly. Over time. I can't just do it without telling her, though"  
"And let me guess, you're struggling to actually go there and say it." Yes, she _was_ running into annoyingly perceptive people. "Honest, I get that. It took me far too long to tell Alice and Patchouli. I suppose you already tried flying to the shrine?"  
"I couldn't get close.", Sanae replied looking very intently at her feet.  
"Wow, you have it bad. Tell you what, I'll do you a favour. Write your message down, and I'll bring it to Reimu."  
"Really? Thank you!" Sanae was surprised, but definitely happy.  
"Of course! I go to the shrine often anyway. She's not going to be in today, something about the Hill of the Nameless, but I can deliver the message tomorrow."  
"Alright. Do you have paper? I didn't think to bring some."  
"Sure, hang on a minute", said the witch as she disappeared inside her fortress of... her house. A shockingly short time later, she was back with paper and a quill pen. "The pen's magical, it won't run outta ink."  
"Convenient", said the shrine maiden distractedly as she tried to sign. She'd been in Gensokyo for a while, but quill pens were still a pain. Eventually, she managed something she thought was decently readable. Marisa didn't comment on it, so apparently it wasn't too bad.  
"Alright then", the witch said, "is that all"?  
"Uh, yes. Thanks again!"  
"No problem. See you around!"  
"See you!" And with that, Sanae was flying towards Yokai Mountain, desperately trying to hold in her elation for just a bit longer, before giving in to fits of giggles and laughter when she thought Marisa was out of hearshot. By the time she reached the shrine, the sky was growing dark, so Sanae landed in, said hi to her adoptive moms, and headed straight to her futon to sleep.  

The next morning, Sanae woke with a start. She had gone to sleep all happy, but the fears had come back during the night and taken the form of nightmares. Fortunately, she'd woken up early. Marisa had to be stopped. What had she been _thinking_ the previous day, writing of all things. This couldn't be allowed to happen. She ran outside with utter disregard for the noise she was making, and as she looked to the sky to take off, she spotted a  painfully familiar black shape riding on a broom. It was all she could do to stare in horror as the black and white witch finished her approach and landed. "Relax, Sanae. It went well. Reimu read the letter, said "whatever", and left it on a table. I've brought it back, I thought you'd not want it to stay there"  
"Oh thank the gods", Sanae said, accidentally out loud. Then, this time intentionally "That... doesn't sound very good, though?"  
"It's Reimu. Coming from her, you just got complete approval to do your thing. It'll be the miracle that changes her mind."  
"What miracle will change Reimu's mind how?" a voice came from behind Sanae, making her startle. This was Suwako, looking onto the young girls with a look that said _I might disapprove of this, but that depends on what you answer._  
"Uh", started Sanae, but she was interrupted by Marisa, who told the goddess about the plan.  
"I see. Honestly, it's not a bad plan.", responded Suwako, causing all of Sanae's newest fears to melt, and be replaced by her previous fears from a few minutes before.  
"It's not? Where am I even going to source that kind of faith? You and Kanako give me some from the shrine, but that won't be enough, and I can hardly ask you to spare your faith for this!"  
"A single miracle, especially one performed over a long period of time, does not actually take all that much faith to accomplish. True, you will need much more than what you have right now, but that's a realistically feasible project."  
"It is?" Sanae was surprised and confused, "How am I going to manage that?"  
"There are many options. The most important thing you need to remember is that faith doesn't have to come in the form of godly worship. It's actually a much broader concept: you could gather faith by attaining significant popularity and a dedicated fanbase, for example"  
"Ohh, like the Prismrivers?" Marisa re-entered the conversation. "They've picked up a new member recently, and they're taking advantage of that to get a lot more people than usual to come listen to them"  
"Like that, yes. The amount of faith required to create a god this way would be absurdly high, of course, but Sanae doesn't have this problem, she's a goddess already. She could build up faith for a while"  
"Wait, you're not seriously suggesting I become a twitch streamer", replied Sanae sarcastically.  
"For all of Kanako's efforts, the technology of Gensokyo isn't there yet. You could become an idol, though", Suwako replied completely seriously.  
"An idol. Really."  
"Really. It would work"  
Marisa interjected again, obviously confused, "Uh, a what?"  
"A dancing and singing girl with some nice music. The Prismrivers could provide the latter, and Sanae is pretty good at the other two. Fancy clothes would definitely help, too", replied Suwako.  
"Ooh. I bet I can get Alice to help with the clothes. She makes clothes for her dolls all the time, she's good at this".  
Meanwhile, Sanae had gone through the five stages of... not grief, but it did have five stages, and she'd reached acceptance. "That would be helpful, yes"  
"Well then, it's settled. The Moriya Shrine is launching their newest idol, Sanae Kochiya, on a secret quest for love. We should speak with Kanako later about organizing this properly." Concluded Suwako.  
"I'll speak with Alice then. When should we meet next?" Asked Marisa, clearly interested.  
"Here in about a week?", replied Suwako.  
"That'll do. See you then!" And with that, the witch was flying away.
